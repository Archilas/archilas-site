## The old way

Lead: Find bits of the past. Stuff them into the prompt. Hope it works.

1. Search — Hunt for whatever seems relevant.
2. Inject everything — Dump it all into the prompt.
3. Token cost — That dump often burns a lot of tokens.
4. No long-term reasoning — There’s no lasting picture to think with.
5. No sense of time — What changed, what’s outdated, what still matters — unclear.

Micro: Search. Paste. Hope.

## How Archilas is different

Lead: Keep a living record. Reason over it when you ask.

1. Compact — Preferences, decisions, open loops — kept, not dumped.
2. Reason — Thinks with that record when it supports the answer.
3. Deliver — Clear answers into the tools you already use.

Micro: Compact. Reason. Deliver.
