# Compare contrast (no demo)

This band is old way vs Archilas. Not a product demo.

## Labels

- Them: `The old way`
- Them line: `You hunt old chats and paste scraps into the prompt.`
- Us: `Archilas`
- Us line: `You keep what matters — and get a clear answer when you ask.`
- Micros: `Search. Paste. Hope.` → `Compact. Reason. Deliver.`
- Under-plate: `Most memory is still paste. Archilas keeps a living record.`

## Old way beats

- Searches for “relevant” snippets
- Injects everything into the prompt
- Often costs many tokens
- No long-term reasoning
- No sense of what changed or what’s still open

## Us beats

- Keeps a living, compacted record
- Reasons over it when it can
- Remembers what changed and what’s still open
- Doesn’t dump the world into every prompt
